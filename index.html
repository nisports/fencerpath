Features

Everything a fencing season actually needs

Built from real problems: missed deadlines, double-booked weekends, and guessing which competition is worth the travel.

Season calendar & planning

Every competition, camp, and deadline for every fencer in one shared calendar — month view for the full picture, timeline view for what's next.

Smart recommendations

FAQ

Good to know

Is my data private?
Yes. Your competition and fencer data is tied to your account and only visible to you — it isn't shared across other users' accounts.
Do I need a credit card to start?
No. The Free plan covers one fencer with no payment details required. You only add payment info if you choose to upgrade later.
Which federations and rankings are supported?
FencerPath currently syncs Swedish national rankings and VM/EM selection rankings automatically, alongside FIE competition data. More federations are being added over time.
Can I use it on my phone?
Yes — FencerPath works in any mobile browser, and can be added to your home screen for an app-like experience.
